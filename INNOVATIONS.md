# ProofCoach AI innovations

## Parser Robustness Lab

A transparent local score derived from extraction, contact fields, section recovery, dates, skills, projects, layout warnings, ordering, and parse confidence. It is explicitly not a universal ATS score.

## Career Evidence Graph

Makes the chain from skill to project/experience, resume claim, demonstrated interview evidence, and job requirement visible. Missing links become targeted gaps.

## Resume Truth Loop

Interview answers strengthen or weaken demonstrated understanding of a claim. “After interview” confidence means stronger demonstrated understanding; it does not prove the underlying real-world event.

## Evidence Lock

Resume rewrites are classified as safe, confirmation required, or blocked. Unsupported metrics and new facts are blocked before text is proposed.

## Adaptive Interview

Answers route to deeper technical questions, clarifications, evidence challenges, trade-offs, or communication follow-ups. The engine persists state and explains why the next question was selected.

## Focus Shield and wellbeing cap

The browser can simplify the in-app interface, mute non-essential app notifications, time work, and autosave. OS notification/call filtering remains a clearly labeled future mobile integration.
